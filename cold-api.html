FAQ

Why not just use ChatGPT?

ChatGPT cannot scrape Linkedin profiles. Context window limitations and lack of live data. Cold securely injects LinkedIn social graph data that standard LLMs cannot access, resulting in messages that actually reference real activity. No hallucinations.

Is it GDPR/CCPA compliant?

Yes. We process public business data and rely on Legitimate Interest for B2B prospecting. We do not store personal data longer than necessary for the outreach campaign.

Is it Linkedin compliant?

Yes, Cold AI is Linkedin compliant. Cold AI is not fully automated, requiring human sign off and approval for every message./p>
