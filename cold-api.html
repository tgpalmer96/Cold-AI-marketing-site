Why Cold API?

Cold API is your direct path to Cold’s outbound intelligence layer. Instead of logging into the Cold web app, call our API to generate and orchestrate outreach directly inside your workflows. Our agents analyse LinkedIn profiles and your product or service, then apply sales psychology to craft context‑aware messages.

Quick Start

Once you have an API key, call our endpoint with a LinkedIn profile URL and a concise description of your offering. Cold returns a tailored opening message you can send immediately.

// Request
POST /v1/messages
{
  "profile_url": "https://www.linkedin.com/in/daniel-example",
  "product": "AI prospecting platform for climate‑tech founders",
  "sender": {
    "name": "Sarah",
    "company": "Your Company",
    "voice": "friendly"
  },
  "tone": "warm",
  "channel": "linkedin"
}

// Response
{
  "message": "Hi Daniel, I really enjoyed your recent post on sustainable supply chains.  At Your Company we help climate‑tech leaders like yourself turn more connections into customers.  Would it make sense to explore how our AI prospecting platform could support your mission?"
}

Why Not the App?

The Cold web app is designed for individuals and small teams who prefer a ready‑made workspace. The API is for builders who want control and scale. With the API you get:

  • No UI dependency – embed intelligence anywhere
  • Fine‑grained control over tone, voice and narrative logic
  • Multi‑tenant usage with per‑client keys and usage tracking
  • Integration hooks for research → messaging → follow‑up flows

What makes Cold different from ChatGPT or Gemini?

Cold integrates with LinkedIn compliantly to read posts, interests and career history. We combine that with a summary of your product to craft messages that feel truly personal.

What data do I need to send?

All you need is a LinkedIn profile URL and a short description of what you offer. You can optionally include tone and voice parameters. Cold’s agent does the research and synthesis for you.

How do you achieve a 4.7× reply rate?

By using real context. Cold messages reference what the prospect cares about and tie it back to your offer. Our sales‑psychology frameworks were developed with experienced sellers and PhD‑level researchers, driving a measured 4.7 × increase in reply rate across customers.

Can I edit or iterate on the outputs?

Yes. Cold returns clear JSON messages you can review, tweak or feed back into the API for refinement. You can also use our app’s conversation mode to chat with the agent for more creative drafts or LinkedIn post writing.

Is my data secure?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never sell or share prospect information. Enterprise customers can request additional privacy controls.

Can I control the tone and brand voice?

Absolutely. Each call lets you specify tone (warm, professional, bold, etc.) and voice (e.g. consultative, visionary). You can also provide your own style prompts to align with your brand.
